There is perhaps no other university or institution as intimately connected with the law and jurisprudence as yale. The university, located in New Haven Connecticut, was ranked as the number one law school by usnews.com's ranking system since 1996. However, it has now been surpassed by Stanford, their fellow ivy league compatriot who had shared the spot with Yale for the past three years and will force Yale to now share the second-place spot with the University of Chicago.

Yale's law school, which boasts a highly prestigious portfolio of alumni including Bill Clinton, Superem Court Justices Clarence Thompson and Samuel Alito and Thompson Reuters CEO Tom Glocer.

The yearly rankings are based off a variety of assessments of universities, including employment, first time bar passing and acceptance rates.

However, this will likely be inconsequential to the future employment of current Yale students as it is still, by a wide margin, considered the best law school globally. That being said, it is equally apparent that Yale must increase their standard to ensure they climb back up to their usual number one spot.
