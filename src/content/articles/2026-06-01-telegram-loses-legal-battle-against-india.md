Telegram, the Russian founded and owned cloud messaging app has lost its appeal in the High Court against a temporary nation-wide ban imposed unto it.

The Indian government blocked the app, which is one of the world's most used messaging apps, due to their concerns that man students were using it to sell leaked exam questions.

The legal loss that Telegram has been handed and its subsequent failure in appeal on the nineteenth of June has sparked widespread outcry from free speech advocates.

However, the banning of Telegram is likely to have greater of an impact that merely stopping the leaking of exams. Over one hundred million Indians use the app, giving rivals such as Signal and WhatsApp an opportunity to increase the market share in the most populated country in the globe. Further, it may be expected that the ban may cause any companies using it primarily for communication to permanently move to their rivals.
