On the first weekend of 2026, the United States of America announced that, after months of tension, they had captured the President of Venezuela, Maduro and his wife and transported them to New York for the former to be tried on Narco-Terrorist charges. Though there has been a frenzy on social media, with various influences advising followers to invest in oil stocks, [**seekingaplha.com**](http://seekingaplha.com/) asserted that any spike will be short. Venezuela sits on the largest oil reserve globally, which will surely be benefit to the United States, whose president, Donald Trump promised that their country would 'run' the nation. The current oversupply of oil means that any short decline in Venezuela's less than a million barrels a day production would be negligible. The clearest winner in this event would be Chevron; it is the only US energy producer located in Venezuela and has over a century of presence in the country, therefore potentially giving it an advantage in acquiring government contracts.
