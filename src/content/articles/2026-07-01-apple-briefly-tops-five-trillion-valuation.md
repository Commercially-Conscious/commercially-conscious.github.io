Perhaps the most culturally relevant and influential technology giant, Apple, reaches new heights on the twenty eighth of July when it passed a five trillion-dollar valuation, only a day after passing Nvidia to become the most valuable publicly traded company.

This is despite worries from investors last year that the company was trailing behind the likes of its competitors such as Meta and Microsoft due to its hesitant policy towards artificial intelligence.

However, it appears that their bet against AI has now paid off as it has given them an advantageous extra spending budget as well as an impressive twenty-five percent rise in stock price.
