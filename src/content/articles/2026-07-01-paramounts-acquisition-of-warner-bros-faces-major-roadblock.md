It seems inevitable at this point that the drama filled saga of the acquisition of Warner Bro's Discovery will be turned into a documentary. The high jacking of Netflix's acquisition of the film company by Paramount Skydance surprised many. However, that was merely the beginning of the deal's theatrics.

In July, a federal judge in California blocked Paramount's acquisition of the company due to fears that the deal will result in an unfair monopoly. The one hundred and ten billion dollar deal would be delayed until August for a hearing against it. Twelve states opposed the deal's signing despite it being approved by the United State's department of Justice. The application was spearheaded by California, whose attorney general, Rob Bonta, claimed that it would lead to "higher prices, lower quality, and less content". This resulted in Paramount agreeing to delay the deal until at least next year.

Paramount also faces challenges in other jurisdictions. In the United Kingdom, officials suggested that there may be potential interventions in the deal whilst the European Union gave the deal it's greenlight on the condition that Paramount ends its distributing deal with Universal.
