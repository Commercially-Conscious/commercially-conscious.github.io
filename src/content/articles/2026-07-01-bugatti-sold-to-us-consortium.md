Reuters reported in April that Porsche was offering its stake in Bugatti to a consortium of US led companies. This likely came as a surprise to many car enthusiast who closely associate the producer of the fastest road worthy car globally with Porsche.

Now, in July, news broke out that the German luxury car manufacturer sold its forty five percent stake in the French car Manufacturer to Hof Capital, a ten-billion-dollar New York based venture capital that is closely associated with the Egyptian billionaire family, the Sawris.

The sale of Bugatti is likely due to a major slump in profit of Porsche of ninety three percent last year. The car brand will now be a part of Hof's portfolio, which boasts companies such as Anthropic, the artificial intelligence giant and multiple of Elon Musk's companies such as Neuralink.
