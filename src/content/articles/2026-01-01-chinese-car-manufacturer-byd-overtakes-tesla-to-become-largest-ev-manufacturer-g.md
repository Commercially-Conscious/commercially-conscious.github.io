On New Years day, Chinese car manufacturer BYD, short for 'Build your Dreams' announced that sales of its electric cars rose by 28 percent to 2.25 million units. This is expected to surpass Tesla's annual production, which analysts assert will likely be 1.65 million units. Tesla faced a challenging 2025 with its owner, Elon Musk, facing significant backlash for his polarizing political activities resulting in a slump in sales. Musk faces a notable task of fulfilling his promise to investors of Tesla to "significantly reduce his role in the Trump administration's DOGE (Department of Government Efficiency) to focus on improving Tesla's sales in the upcoming year.
