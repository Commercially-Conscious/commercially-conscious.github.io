One would be forgiven to find out that Shell Oil Company informed Reuters in December of 2025 that they were pausing their offshore wind investments and that BP likewise announced in February 2025 that they would be returning to primarily oil and gas production whilst simultaneously cutting investments in renewable energy sector. This is particularly surprising considering the rise in environmental consciousness in the past century, especially amongst Gen-Z, with faces such as Greta Thunberg pioneering anti-nonrenewable rhetoric. A plethora of factors have led both energy giants top move away from sustainable energy production with falling profits in the recent years as well as reducing expenditure. Unsurprisingly, this has invoked anger amongst environmental organizations such as Greenpeace as well as commercial figure, with former Barclays Bank executive Sir Ian Cheshire informing the BBC's Today program that he "wonder[s] whether this sort of decision will look right in 10 years, "noting that "the climate change issue has not gone away, the science hasn't changed."
