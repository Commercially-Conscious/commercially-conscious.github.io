The historic and blockbuster one hundred and eleven billion dollar acquisition of Warner Brothers by Paramount Skydance faced a serious roadblock this year when the United State's Department of Justice launched lengthy investigations against Paramount, which was likely fueled by concerns from opposition, who argued that the acquisition would create an illegal monopoly.

The investigation lasted over eight months and included reviews of over two million documents. Paramount were therefore likely relieved to hear on the twelfth of June that the DOJ approved of the acquisition, clarifying that it would not violate any antitrust law, even stating that it may in fact increase competition against that of other streaming services, namely Netflix.

The road to the acquisition of Warner Brothers Discovery has perhaps been one of the most talked-about and dramatic in recent M&A history. The film giant initially agreed to be acquired by Netflix before Paramount poached the former.

However, there are still several issues that will have to be navigated before the acquisition is fully completed; it is expected that several states such as California as well as the European Union will launch separate suits and investigations against it.
